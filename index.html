Current Focus

vLLM Inference Runtime on AMD ROCm MI300X

Two structured MI300X benchmarking studies — a dense model and a MoE/latent-attention model. The method is the same for every experiment: measure a bottleneck, attribute it to a hardware resource, change a software knob, and prove the result with rocprofv3, Omniperf, and rocm-smi.

Study 1 · Dense Architecture
Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct on MI300X
Baseline → concurrency → KV-cache exhaustion → FP8 → AITER → composition → roofline. Throughput scales 192.6 → 5,595 tok/s (29×) via concurrency, FP8 adds +55% at the knee, AITER is ~neutral on this dense architecture.

Study 2 · MoE + Latent Attention
DeepSeek-V2-Lite on MI300X
Same methodology on a MoE + MLA architecture (15.7B total / 2.4B active). AITER becomes the single largest lever (+38.7%), decomposed into MoE-routing vs. MLA-attention contributions via a 4-arm ablation.
