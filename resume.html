vLLM Inference Runtime on AMD ROCm MI300X (AMD Developer Cloud · vLLM · ROCm)
Study 1 — Dense Architecture (Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, BF16/FP8)
  • Ran a full benchmarking pipeline on a single MI300X — baseline, concurrency sweep, KV-cache breaking point, FP8, AITER, composition, and roofline profiling — scaling throughput 192.6 → 5,595 tok/s (29×) via concurrency alone, with FP8 adding a further 1.55× at peak load for +0.34% perplexity cost.
  • Used roofline / arithmetic-intensity analysis to predict, ahead of measurement, that AITER would give ~0% benefit on this dense architecture (compute <1% utilized at low concurrency; AITER's kernels target MoE/MLA models, not standard attention) — confirmed empirically (-0.1% measured).
  • Identified a KV-cache exhaustion cliff at concurrency 256 (ISL=2048) where p99 TTFT jumps >50% in a single step from vLLM preemption/recompute — established as the hard SLO boundary independent of raw throughput headroom.
  • Ran a composition test stacking FP8 + AITER: +32.5% vs. BF16, sub-additive against the +54.8% naive sum of individual gains — showing both optimizations relieve the same HBM-bandwidth bottleneck.
  • Captured a long-prompt (ISL=7,680) roofline point at arithmetic intensity >7,680 FLOP/byte, confirming prefill is compute-bound — the architectural inverse of memory-bound decode, motivating disaggregated prefill/decode serving.
Study 2 — MoE / Latent-Attention Architecture (DeepSeek-V2-Lite)
  • Repeated the methodology on DeepSeek-V2-Lite (MoE + MLA, 15.7B total / 2.4B active per step); designed a 4-arm leave-one-out AITER ablation (full / no-MoE / no-MLA / off) to isolate kernel-level contributions, verified against rocprofv3 kernel traces.
  • Found AITER to be the single largest performance lever on this architecture (+38.7% over BF16, 9,697 tok/s peak) — decomposed into +31.6% from MLA-attention kernels vs. +16.6% from MoE routing, the architecture-dependent inverse of the dense-model result — cross-checked with rocprof-compute roofline profiling and PMC hardware counters.
